# Interview guide (20–30 minutes)

For Abu. The goal is to learn about their problem, not to pitch. Ask about the past, not about what they might do. Take notes in their words. Show the product only at the end, if there is time.

## Before

- Ask permission to take notes, and ask whether they can be quoted by name or only by role.
- Note the date, their role, and their organisation type and size.

## 1. Context (5 min)

1. What do you trade, and on which venues?
2. Walk me through the last time you took a position on a short, known event: a data release, a daily close, a weekend.
3. How did you size it? What stopped you going bigger?

## 2. The leak (10 min)

4. Have you ever changed a trade because someone could see it? What happened? *(H1)*
5. Have you used Polymarket or Kalshi? What did you notice about who could see your positions?
6. Have you been copied or front-run, or do you know someone who has? What did it cost?
7. How do you hide size today? What does that cost you in time, fills or fees?
8. From 1 to 5, how much does position visibility hurt you? Why that number?

## 3. How the venue works (5 min)

9. Would you trade against a venue that sees your trade, as an OTC desk does? What would make you trust it? *(H3)*
10. How do you check that a market resolved fairly? Is a price from named exchanges that you can recompute enough? *(H4)*
11. What fees do you pay now per trade, all in? *(H5)*
12. Which windows matter to you: minutes, hours or a day? Which events? *(H2)*

## 4. Close (5 min)

13. If this existed with real money through a licensed operator, who at your firm would have to say yes?
14. Would you try a demo seat now and tell me what is wrong with it? *(Pilot signal)*
15. Who else should I talk to?
