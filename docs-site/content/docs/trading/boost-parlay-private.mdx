> **Planned on Canton.** This page describes how each of these works in Agari. Their Daml contracts are not on the ledger yet, so on the Canton test network they cannot take a stake yet.

## Boost

In the <AppLink href="/markets">Markets ticket</AppLink>, choose the ordinary route to see 2× or 3× Boost when available. The reserve fronts the rest of the position; your loss is capped at your stake, but the position may knock out before expiry. Review exposure, knock-out line, charge and live quote. Existing Boosts appear in <AppLink href="/portfolio">Portfolio</AppLink>, where cash-out, settlement and claim depend on their state. A Private ticket cannot be boosted.

## Parlay

At <AppLink href="/parlay">Parlay</AppLink>, combine two or three Window legs into one ticket. Every leg must win for the combined payout; one miss loses the ticket. A venue void can void the ticket under its rules. The quote accounts for legs that share a closing print, and the reserve prefunds the maximum payout. Read all legs, the final quote and reserve availability before signing; follow the ticket through settlement and claim on the same route.

## Private

Private is a separate **1×** route inside the <AppLink href="/markets">Markets ticket</AppLink>. It has its own balance and claim flow in <AppLink href="/portfolio">Portfolio</AppLink>. On Canton every position is already visible only to your seat and the venue, so Private's promise holds without extra machinery; its route is planned. A Private bet cannot use Boost. The presence of Private in a selector does not by itself confirm that the route is usable on the current deployment.

All three use demo credits, and all three are **planned** on Canton. For the ordinary call and its opening and closing prints, see [Place a trade](/trading/first-trade).
