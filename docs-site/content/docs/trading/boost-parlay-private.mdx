> **Boost and Parlay run on a local Canton sandbox so far:** each was placed from its screen and settled there. Private is not on Canton. None of the three is on a hosted test network yet.

## Boost

In the <AppLink href="/markets">Markets ticket</AppLink>, choose 2× or 3× Boost when it is offered. The Boost reserve fronts the rest of the position and takes a premium; your loss is capped at your stake and you never owe anything. The quote pins a knock-out line on the underlying price. The venue's keeper can knock a position out only when the Window's own oracle quorum has printed at or beyond that line, never on its own say, and a 1× position has no line. Review exposure, line, premium and quote before accepting. Existing Boosts appear in <AppLink href="/portfolio">Portfolio</AppLink>: **Cash out** takes the venue's firm buy-back price, and a settled Boost pays straight into your cash. The knock-out and the stale refund are proven by the Daml tests; neither has been triggered in a local run yet.

## Parlay

At <AppLink href="/parlay">Parlay</AppLink>, combine two or three Window legs into one ticket. Every leg must win for the combined payout; one miss kills the ticket, and a voided Window voids it and returns your stake. Legs are decided in the order their Windows close. The quote accounts for legs that share a closing print, and the reserve locks the maximum payout at open, so a win is always paid in full. Read all legs, the final quote and reserve availability before accepting; the ticket stays on the same route through settlement, and a receipt keeps its result after it ends.

## Private

Private was a separate **1×** route with its own balance and claim flow. It is not on Canton: every position here is already a contract that only your seat and the venue can see, so there is no second balance to fund. If the ticket still shows a Private option, it answers that Private mode is not available.

For the ordinary call and its opening and closing prints, see [Place a trade](/trading/first-trade).
