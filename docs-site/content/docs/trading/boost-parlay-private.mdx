These paths use DevNet test credits and require their own available contracts, balances and quotes. Read the route's current state before funding or placing a ticket.

## Boost

In an ordinary Markets ticket, choose an offered Boost multiple. The reserve fronts part of the position and charges a premium. Review the cost, exposure, premium and knock-out line. Your stake caps your loss.

An open Boost can be sold back when the venue offers an exit quote. Its knock-out requires the Window's oracle quorum at the pinned line. Settlement follows the Window's Resolution, with the reserve repaid before you. [Short](/trading/shorts) uses the same reserve for Down positions.

## Parlay

At <AppLink href="/parlay">Parlay</AppLink>:

1. Add two or three eligible Window legs and choose each side.
2. Enter a stake and review every leg, the final quote and maximum payout.
3. Confirm only while the quote is usable and reserve capacity is available.
4. Follow **Your tickets** as each Window resolves.

Every leg must win. One loss loses the ticket. A voided leg voids the ticket and returns the stake. The reserve locks the maximum payout when a DevNet ticket opens. The Trade screen's parlay controls also support guest paper tickets; those do not create a Canton ticket.

**Hosted check — 8 October 2026:** the builder quoted a two-leg AAPL Up / MSFT Down 15-minute ticket for a one-credit stake, with a 2.89 payout and correlation note. **Place** was not clicked. The walkthrough shows a quote preview; it does not establish placement or settlement.

## Private

Private mode is an ordinary **1×** call paid from your seat's private balance. Open **Private** in Portfolio's pockets to inspect its balance and move test credits in or out. When the ticket reports Private as available, select it and review the amount before confirming.

The current contracts tag the seat's leg as private and return settlement or refund money to its private bucket. Private calls stay off the public record and leaderboard. **The venue still sees the call as counterparty.** This is not anonymity from the operator, and a private balance is separate from the basket [desk](/agents/desk).

If Private reports a missing capability, wait for that capability rather than treating a visible control as a successful private trade. See [Balances](/trading/balances) and [availability](/help/availability).
