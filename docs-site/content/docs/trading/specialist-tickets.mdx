title: "Other ways to trade"
description: "Compare Range, Moonshot, Boost, Short, Parlay and Private before opening their ticket."
---

Each path changes the ordinary Window ticket. All funded positions here use test credits with no cash value. A route needs its own enabled contracts, current quote and, where applicable, reserve capacity.

| Path | What you choose | What decides the result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Range](/trading/range-and-moonshot) | Inside or outside a stated band | The Window's closing print relative to the band |
| [Moonshot](/trading/range-and-moonshot) | Long or short and a strike | The close reaching the quoted level |
| [Boost](/trading/boost-parlay-private) | A side and offered multiple | The Window result, or an oracle-backed knock-out |
| [Short](/trading/shorts) | A reserve-backed Down position | The exit quote, knock-out or Window result |
| [Parlay](/trading/boost-parlay-private) | Two or three Window legs | All legs winning; a void refunds the ticket |
| [Private](/trading/boost-parlay-private) | An ordinary 1× call from a private pocket | The same Window result, paid into the private balance |

Read the actual quote for cost, payout and limits. A reserved payout does not mean a guaranteed win. A route showing **not deployed**, no quote or a paused reserve cannot accept a new ticket. [Status](/help/status) and [availability](/help/availability) explain the checks.
