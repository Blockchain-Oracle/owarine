Open either mode from <AppLink href="/games">Games</AppLink>. Each needs a funded seat, an eligible trading Window, a usable quote and reserve capacity. If the page names a missing contract or source, placement is unavailable even though the route opens.

## Range

At <AppLink href="/games/range">Range</AppLink>, choose a Window, set the band's centre and width, then choose **Inside** or **Outside**. Enter a stake and review the quoted band and payout before placing. The closing print decides the result. The quote comes from the reserve's pricing; a narrow band does not imply one fixed payout multiple, and an almost certain band may be refused.

## Moonshot

At <AppLink href="/games/moonshot">Moonshot</AppLink>, choose long or short and an offered target multiple. Review the solved strike, stake and payout. Long wins at or above its strike; short wins at or below it. Confirm the quote and follow **Your rounds**.

## After placement

The DevNet round holds the stake and locks the reserve's quoted payout. The keeper normally settles it after the Window's Resolution. A miss loses the stake; a void returns it. Follow any claim or stale-refund action the round offers if the normal settlement has not completed.

A quote or draft alone is not an accepted round. Check its receipt and balance before submitting again after an uncertain result. See [Print proof](/trading/proof) for the signed price behind the Window.
