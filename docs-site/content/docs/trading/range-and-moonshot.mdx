> **Planned on Canton.** This page describes how Range and Moonshot work in Agari. Their Daml contracts are not on the ledger yet, so on the Canton test network they cannot take a stake yet.

<AppLink href="/games/range">Range</AppLink> asks whether the closing price will land **inside or outside** a band you set. Choose a live Window, move the band or its width, choose a side, and inspect the quoted stake and payout before placing. A narrow band may pay more, but the quote comes from the venue book and its measured movement, so read the actual ticket rather than assuming a fixed multiple.

<AppLink href="/games/moonshot">Moonshot</AppLink> asks whether the close reaches a level. Choose long or short and a target multiple; the reserve solves the strike from the live basis. The ticket shows the strike, stake and payout before you place it. Long pays at or above its level; short pays at or below its level.

Both modes use the same signed closing print as the underlying Window. The reserve sets aside the full potential payout at placement. After resolution, use **Your rounds** to settle or claim. A void returns the stake; a missed outcome loses it. A live Window, usable quote and reserve capacity are required, so the route can be present while placement is unavailable.

For an ordinary Up/Down call, see [Place a trade](/trading/first-trade). For what is on the ledger today, see [The Daml engine](/architecture/programs).
