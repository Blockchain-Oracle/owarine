> **On a local Canton sandbox so far.** A Range and a Moonshot ticket have each been placed from their screens, settled and kept as receipts there. Neither is on a hosted test network yet.

<AppLink href="/games/range">Range</AppLink> asks whether the closing price will land **inside or outside** a band you set. Choose a live Window, move the band or its width, choose a side, and inspect the quoted stake and payout before placing. A narrow band may pay more, but the quote comes from the reserve's pricing around the Window's opening print and its measured movement, so read the actual ticket rather than assuming a fixed multiple. A nearly certain band is refused.

<AppLink href="/games/moonshot">Moonshot</AppLink> asks whether the close reaches a level. Choose long or short and a target multiple; the reserve solves the strike from the live basis. The ticket shows the strike, stake and payout before you place it. Long pays at or above its level; short pays at or below its level.

Both modes use the same signed closing print as the underlying Window. Accepting the firm quote creates a round only you and the venue can see, holding your stake and the reserve's lock, so the full payout is set aside at placement. After resolution the venue's keeper settles it into your cash; if it has not by the refund time, **Your rounds** lets you claim or take your stake back yourself. A void returns the stake; a missed outcome loses it. A live Window, a usable quote and reserve capacity are required, so the route can be present while placement is unavailable.

For an ordinary Up/Down call, see [Place a trade](/trading/first-trade). For the contracts behind a round, see [The Daml packages](/architecture/programs).
