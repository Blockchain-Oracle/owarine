---
title: "Halts and voids"
description: "Read an entry pause separately from a Window's final void reason."
---

A pause stops new entry. A **void** is a recorded Window result. Ordinary voided calls return their stake and fee rather than choosing an unsupported Up or Down outcome.

## Void reasons

| Reason | What happened |
| --- | --- |
| Missing print | No valid print arrived for the required boundary by its deadline. |
| Quorum not met | Some prints arrived, but fewer than the required oracle quorum. |
| Resolver absent | A quorum existed but was not resolved before the deadline. |
| Source disagreement | The submitted prices exceeded the Series' agreement limit, or event attestations conflicted. |

A Window records one Resolution. Open [Print proof](/trading/proof) for its specific reason and evidence. The usual price Series needs two of three oracle parties within its configured spread limit; the Window's registered terms remain authoritative.

## Trading halted or signed price stale

**Trading halted** identifies an issuer's halt signal. **Signed price stale** identifies a source that is no longer fresh enough. The watcher uses each lane's own registered source; it does not substitute an unrelated chart price.

The roller can stop listing new Windows and the pricer can pull quotes while a lane is paused. An existing Window still resolves or voids under its own print deadlines. A closed regular stock session is expected and is separate from either condition.

## Follow the refund

The settler normally pays an ordinary void refund into your seat's cash. Private calls return to their private bucket. Specialist tickets have their own refund receipts; read [Range and Moonshot](/trading/range-and-moonshot) or [Boost and Parlay](/trading/boost-parlay-private).

If the result or payment is late, inspect the resolver, settler and projection checks on [Status](/help/status). Use the claim or stale-refund action offered for the position rather than assuming a missing display means the funds were lost.
