<AppLink href="/baskets">Baskets</AppLink> groups the eight supported PreStocks names into five choices. Each basket is an **equal-weight price-return index**: it started at 1,000 points at a frozen set of base prices read from the PreStocks catalogue on 22 September 2026. Each oracle party computes it from all members in one catalogue read; a missing member means no index print, never a partial one. The points are an index level, **not a dollar price or a token you buy**.

| Basket | Members |
| --- | --- |
| AI Labs (`AILABS`) | OpenAI, Anthropic |
| Frontier AI (`FRONTIER`) | OpenAI, Anthropic, Figure AI, Neuralink |
| Prediction Markets (`PREDMKTS`) | Kalshi, Polymarket |
| Defense & Space (`DEFSPACE`) | Anduril, SpaceX |
| All PreStocks (`PREALL`) | All eight names |

Each card keeps the same rows whatever loads or rolls: the members as a stack of logos with their count ("2 companies · equal weight"), the index in points with its recent range and its move this week, and the live Window with its countdown and Up and Down prices in cents. A Window nobody is quoting says **No quotes yet**. Basket Windows trade 24/7, so a card never shows the stock market's session.

## Pick the action you mean

| Action on the card | Where it goes | Money and network |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Predict** | The basket's one-hour Up/Down Window, or its ticker page if none is trading | Demo credits; basket Windows have opened and resolved on a local Canton sandbox |
| **Cover** | A Down call against a basket when you hold at least two of its members; one member uses its own name | **Not on Canton yet**: a seat holds no outside tokens until the Canton Coin and CIP-56 holdings rail is wired, so Cover stays unavailable |
| **Hold** | The [desk studio](/agents/desk) with that basket preselected | A practice desk on paper first; see [Build a desk](/agents/desk) for going live |

The basket Window settles on the change in **points** between two boundary prints. At each boundary the three oracle parties each read the PreStocks catalogue once, compute the index, and post it; a quorum of two within 1% of each other resolves the Window, and the receipt names "the PreStocks basket index" as the source. That is a venue-attested PreStocks value, not an independent check; see [PreStocks and Pyth](/architecture/prestocks-and-pyth). When PreStocks refuses the venue's reads (it rate-limits some hosts), the basket lanes pause rather than print. The [source map](/builders/source-map) points to the index code.

If the card says **No Window trading right now**, you can still inspect its members and draft a desk. A Predict order needs a live Window and a quote.
