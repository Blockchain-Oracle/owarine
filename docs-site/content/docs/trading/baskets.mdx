| AI Labs | OpenAI, Anthropic |

| Prediction Markets | Kalshi, Polymarket |
| Defense & Space | Anduril, SpaceX |
| All PreStocks | All eight names |

The displayed basket level is an index in **points**, not a dollar price or an equity token. It starts from 1,000 at frozen member base prices captured on 22 September 2026. Each index read uses all its members from one PreStocks catalogue response; a missing member makes the read incomplete.

## Predict or automate

**Predict** opens a basket's available Up/Down Window. Review the signed index line, side, stake and quote before accepting. The Window resolves on its opening and closing index prints. No Window or **No quotes yet** means you cannot place an immediate basket call.

**Automate** opens the [desk studio](/agents/desk) with that basket selected. It creates a paper practice desk first; drafting does not buy a basket or enable trades.

Baskets have a 24/7 schedule, but still need their configured Series, fresh PreStocks reads and venue quotes. The source is a venue-attested PreStocks index. It is not an independent company valuation. See [PreStocks and Pyth](/architecture/prestocks-and-pyth) and [Print proof](/trading/proof).
