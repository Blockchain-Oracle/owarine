---

Portfolio is where a position's life ends up: a call you accepted, or a ticket such as Range, Parlay or Boost. It reads the ledger as your seat's party, from the start of your lease, so it shows exactly what Canton lets your seat see and never a previous visitor's positions.

## Open positions

An open position sits on a live Window. A quote you have not accepted yet is not a position; it expires on its own after about 20 seconds. Before the close, **Cash out** or **Sell half** sells a position back to the venue at a firm exit price, and the proceeds land in your cash.

## Settled positions

Once a Window resolves (or voids), the venue's settler pays its positions in batches, without you signing. A winning position pays its full amount; a losing position pays nothing; a voided Window returns your stake and your fee. You do not need to claim. History shows each result with its receipt, an event reads as its question, and each receipt opens its Window's proof page.

If the venue has not settled by the Window's refund time, Portfolio offers to take your stake back yourself.

## If a result looks stuck

Right after a Window closes, the resolver and the settler need a moment. <AppLink href="/status">Status</AppLink> shows whether the oracle feeders, the resolver and the settler are running. A Window whose prints never arrive voids rather than hanging forever.
