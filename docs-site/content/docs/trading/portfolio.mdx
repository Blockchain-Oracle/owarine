## Read from top to bottom

| Section | What to check |
| --- | --- |
| Your balance | Test credits, open positions valued at the venue mid, and amounts to collect. **Get test credits** opens Add funds; **Trade** opens the trading screen. |
| Pockets | Trading Balance, Private and X replies, with their own balances and controls. |
| Open positions | Accepted positions and resting calls, including any available exit or cancellation action. |
| To collect | Positions for which an owner action is available. |
| Record | Settled history and its receipts. |
| Trader Edge | A report derived from your trading record. |

A current mark is not a guaranteed sale price. A quote you have not accepted is not an open position. Some sections load after the balance; wait for their read or use their retry state.

## Sell a position back

1. Open an ordinary position before its close.
2. Choose **Cash out** or **Sell half**, where offered. The Trade screen uses **Close** and reduction controls for its positions.
3. Review the venue's firm exit quote and confirm.
4. Wait for the receipt and the updated position size or cash balance.

The venue must be quoting an exit at your size. A missing or expired quote cannot close the position. Specialist tickets have their own close rules; see [Short](/trading/shorts) and [other tickets](/trading/specialist-tickets).

## After the Window closes

A winning ordinary leg pays its contract amount; a losing leg pays zero; a void refunds its stake and fee. The settler normally sends these payments without another seat signature. History links the result to its receipt and Window proof.

In the hosted check on 8 October 2026, the BTC 2-minute Up call showed **WON**, 3.00 credits paid and +2.15 net, with the payout already reflected in the rail balance. A **TO COLLECT · COLLECT →** label remained beside that paid result. Check the settlement receipt and current balance before attempting collection; that label alone does not mean a second payment is owed.
