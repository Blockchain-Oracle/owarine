---
title: "Portfolio"
description: "Read your open and settled positions, and see what you were paid."
---

Portfolio is where a position's life ends up: a call you accepted, or a call scheduled before its Window opened. It reads the ledger as your seat's party, so it shows exactly what Canton lets your seat see.

## Open positions

An open position sits on a live Window. A quote you have not accepted yet is not a position; it expires on its own after a few seconds.

## Settled positions

Once a Window resolves (or voids), the venue's settler pays its positions in batches. A winning position pays its full amount; a losing position pays nothing; a voided Window returns your stake and your fee. You do not need to claim. History shows each result with its receipt, and each receipt opens its Window's proof page.

If the venue has not settled by the Window's refund time, Portfolio offers to take your stake back yourself.

## If a result looks stuck

Right after a Window closes, the resolver and the settler need a moment. <AppLink href="/status">Status</AppLink> shows whether the oracle feeders, the resolver and the settler are running. A Window whose prints never arrive voids rather than hanging forever.
