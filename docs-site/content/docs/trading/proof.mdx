<Walkthrough name="proof" />

Open <AppLink href="/proof">Print proof</AppLink> from **More → Records and proof**. Choose a decided Window, then inspect its opening and closing evidence, median, spread, outcome or void reason. The result uses those recorded boundary prints, not the latest price on the chart.

## Follow the timeline

1. Check the asset, cadence and Window times.
2. Read each oracle party's open and close print and its named source.
3. Compare the median and spread with the Series' quorum and agreement limit.
4. Read the Resolution and the parties that signed it.
5. Open the ledger update link for the step you want to inspect.

<GuideCapture name="proofTimeline" />

A Resolution establishes the Window's result. Your payout has its own settlement receipt in [Portfolio](/trading/portfolio).

**Hosted check — 8 October 2026:** a settled BTC Window showed its signed exchange prints. Its update link opened a page with the ledger offset, UTC time and witnessed events. That page is Owarine's hosted projection of the update, not an independent block explorer.

## Re-verify

**Re-verify** asks the server to recompute the medians, spreads and outcome, compare archived source bytes with their hashes, and re-fetch supported exchange candles. Read every check: **Pass**, **Fail** and **Unavailable** have different meanings. A missing archived payload cannot be checked merely because its hash is on the ledger.

The proof page also states the trust boundary. Canton limits contract visibility by stakeholder, while this demo's operator holds ledger access for the demo parties. Three oracle parties are not automatically three independent operators. See [price sources](/architecture/price-sources) for what each source proves.
