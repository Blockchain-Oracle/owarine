---
title: "Pre-open calls"
description: "What you can do with a Window before it opens, and why a resting pre-open call is not on Canton yet."
---

> **Not on Canton yet.** Resting a call on a Window before it opens is planned: on Canton it becomes a resting call between you and the venue, filled at the open. That contract is not on the ledger yet, so today the pre-open ticket refuses the call before anything is signed or held.

Because Windows are prelisted ahead of time (see [Sessions and lanes](/trading/sessions-and-lanes)), you can see a Window before the market — or the Monday Gap, or a Window's own trading start — actually opens: its ticker, its length, its opening time and how it will settle. What you cannot do yet is leave a call on it that fills at the bell.

## Where to schedule one

While the stock market is closed, the `/markets` board lists each company's next Windows under **Schedule a call**. Choose a Window length, then **Call Up** or **Call Down** to open the pre-open ticket, which asks for your own price per contract. On <AppLink href="/short">Short</AppLink>, a stock Window that opens later offers **Schedule a Down call** on the same terms. On Canton the ticket's final button cannot be pressed: it reads **Resting calls aren't on Canton yet — calls open at the bell**, so no command reaches the ledger and no credits move.

## How it works

A Window before its trading start is in a **Listed** state. The venue does not quote a Listed Window: there is no firm quote to accept until the Window opens, so an ordinary Up or Down call has to wait for the bell. Canton has no order book to rest an order on either. The venue is the house, and every fill is a firm quote it issues to one seat, so a pre-open call needs its own contract: your stake held against the venue's promise to fill at your price or better once the Window opens.

## What happens at the open

Once the Window opens for trading, the venue starts quoting it and the ordinary ticket works: ask for a firm quote, accept it, and your position exists. A call placed a few seconds after the bell is the closest thing to a pre-open call today.

- **At the bell:** the first quote appears when the venue's pricer has the Window's opening print and a price; until then the card reads **No quotes yet**.
- **The Monday Gap** is different only in timing: it is listed two days ahead, trades from Friday's close, and takes calls until it locks on Sunday at 20:00 ET, well before Monday's bell.

## What's guaranteed and what isn't

- Nothing fills before the open boundary: the venue issues no quote for a Listed Window.
- No fill is promised at the open either. A quote is the venue's offer at its price, for the size its ladder can take.
- If the Window voids for any reason, every call on it gets its stake and fee back.
