title: "Sessions and lanes"
description: "Tell a listed Window from a trading one, and use the right session and price source."
---

A **lane** defines an asset's schedule and price basis. The app can support a lane without the deployment currently listing or quoting it. Read the Window's state, source and countdown before placing a call.

| Lane | Schedule | What its price represents |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Regular stocks and ETFs | The configured US exchange calendar | The source registered for that stock Window |
| Gap | A registered close-to-next-open interval across a weekend or holiday | The Window's two session boundary prints |
| Crypto | Round the clock | Exchange candle closes from the registered oracle parties |
| Tokenized stocks | Round the clock where enabled | The token's source price, not the regular stock tape |
| Pre-IPO and baskets | Round the clock where enabled | PreStocks token prices or the basket index |
| Events | The question's own deadline | Committee attestations under its event rules |

The source and contract parameters on each Window determine its result. See [price sources](/architecture/price-sources) for those boundaries. A headline, assistant read or chart tick is not a settlement input.

## Follow the session

Regular US hours are normally **09:30–16:00 ET**, with holidays and early closes supplied by the venue's calendar. UTC times shift with daylight saving time; use the next-open time shown by the app. A closed stock session does not stop eligible crypto or other 24/7 Windows.

Window length options come from the Series listed on this deployment. Do not assume a ticker has every cadence, or that an enabled cadence always has an enterable Window.

## Listed, trading and locked

- **Listed:** the Window is visible before its trading start. An immediate order waits; an available [pre-open call](/trading/pre-open-calls) can rest at your price.
- **Trading:** entry also requires a usable quote and enough time before the entry cutoff.
- **Locked or closed:** no new entry; resolution and settlement follow their deadlines.
- **Paused or no quotes:** read the reason. A 24/7 schedule does not override a stale source, disabled venue or absent quote.

The roller can list Windows ahead of their boundaries. A new Window does not promise uninterrupted quoting across the transition. [Status](/help/status) shows roller, pricer, source and resolver checks.
