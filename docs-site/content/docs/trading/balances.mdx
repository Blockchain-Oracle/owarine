## Demo credits are test money

Every stake and ticket on Agari is sized in **demo credits**: the venue's test money on a Canton test network. They have no market value and cannot be moved off the venue. A new seat is funded with them when you take it; see [Your seat and demo credits](/start/wallet).

## Where your credits sit

Your credits are cash contracts signed by the venue and your seat's party, so only the two of you can see them. Accepting a quote spends from them: the stake and the fee move into your position, and any change comes back as a new cash contract. When a Window settles, the venue pays the result back into your cash. There is no separate vault to deposit into, and no network fee to keep a balance for.

Nothing about where the money sits changes how a position settles: Up if the close is at or above the open, and a void returns your stake and your fee.

## When a seat is released

Before a released seat is reused, its open positions close at what they cost and its cash is swept back to the venue, so the next visitor starts empty. Your balance does not follow you to a new seat; a new seat starts from fresh demo credits.
