## Demo credits are test money

Every stake and ticket on Agari is sized in **demo credits**: the venue's test money on Canton. They have no cash value and cannot be moved off the venue. The venue credits a seat once per lease, 1,000 demo credits by default, when you take it; see [Your seat and demo credits](/start/wallet).

## Where your credits sit

Your credits are cash contracts signed by both the venue and your seat's party, so neither can create or seize them alone, and only the two of you can see them. Accepting a quote spends from them: the stake and the fee move into your position, and any change comes back as a new cash contract. When a Window settles, the venue pays the result back into your cash. On Canton your seat's cash **is** your Trading Balance: there is nothing to deposit, and no network fee to keep a balance for. The one thing that sets cash aside is a grant: [copying a strategy](/agents/copy-strategies) moves a budget into a grant its runner may spend inside your caps, and revoking returns the rest.

Nothing about where the money sits changes how a position settles: Up if the close is at or above the open, and a void returns your stake and your fee.

## When a seat is released

Before a released seat is reused, its open positions close at what they cost and its cash is swept back to the venue, so the next visitor starts empty. Your balance does not follow you to a new seat; a new seat starts from fresh demo credits.

**Not on Canton yet:** a Private balance and a real-money rail on Canton Coin. See [Portfolio](/trading/portfolio) for where a settled position shows up.
