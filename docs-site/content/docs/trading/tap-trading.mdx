On the earlier version of Agari, tap-trading was an opt-in: a separate session key, a deposit and a fee sponsor, so that each order did not need its own approval popup. On Canton none of that is needed.

## Why a seat is already one tap

Your seat's signing key lives in this browser and signs each call directly, with no popup and no extension. Canton charges no network fee, so there is no fee payer, no sponsor and no budget to top up. The app's fast-mode chip says so rather than offering a setup that does nothing.

## What stays the same

- Each call is still a firm quote you accept, with the exact cost shown first.
- Your positions are yours: only your seat's party and the venue can see them, and the venue cannot accept a quote for you.
- **Reset seat** deletes the key; nothing else can sign for your seat.
