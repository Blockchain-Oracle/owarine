On the earlier version of Owarine, tap-trading was an opt-in: a separate session key, a deposit and a fee sponsor, so that each order did not need its own approval popup. On Canton none of that is needed.

## Why a seat is already one tap

Your seat's signing key lives in this browser (or in the iPhone's Keychain) and signs each call directly, with no popup and no extension. Canton charges no network fee, so there is no fee payer, no sponsor, no second key and no budget to top up. On the web and on the phone, the ticket's chip reads **one tap** and cannot be pressed, because there is nothing to arm.

## What stays the same

- Each call is still a firm quote you accept, with the exact cost shown first.
- Your positions are yours: each one has two stakeholders, your seat's party and the venue, and the venue cannot accept a quote for you.
- **Reset seat** deletes the key; nothing else can sign for your seat.

**Agent grants are a different thing.** Letting a published strategy place calls for you moves a budget into an `AgentGrant` with caps you set, and the strategy's runner places inside them; the ledger checks the caps, not the runner. That works on a local Canton sandbox today. See [Copy a strategy](/agents/copy-strategies).
