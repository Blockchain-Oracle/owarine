---
title: "Tap-trading"
description: "Your seat signs a reviewed order directly; automation needs its own funded permission."
---

Your browser's seat key signs trading requests without an extension popup. There is no separate tap-trading session key to arm. Read the stake, quote and account before confirming; one tap can still spend DevNet test credits.

A signature authorizes a request. A fill is established by the accepted position and its receipt. If the result is uncertain, read [Portfolio](/trading/portfolio) before repeating it. **Reset seat** removes this browser's signing key; it does not convert or transfer the old lease's balance.

[Copy grants](/agents/copy-strategies), [desk mandates](/agents/desk) and [X trading permissions](/explore/trade-from-x) are separate authorizations. They allocate a balance or bounds to a named runner. Creating a strategy or taking a seat alone does not authorize automated trades.
