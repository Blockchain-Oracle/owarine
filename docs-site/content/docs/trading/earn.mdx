> **On a local Canton sandbox so far.** The maker vault (supply, quotes from its own cash, settle, withdraw) and the Range & Moonshot reserve (supply and withdraw) have run end to end there. Earn is not on a hosted test network yet.

<AppLink href="/earn">Earn</AppLink> has four demo-credit reserve tabs. Each has its own shares, exposure and withdrawal capacity. Supplier capital is at risk; none of the tabs promises a fixed yield. These positions are separate from your [balance](/trading/balances) and from the [desk](/agents/desk).

| Tab | What supplier capital backs | What may keep it committed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Maker vault | Quotes on both sides of the venue's own Windows, drawn from the vault's cash; the spread earned and inventory losses change share value. | Quotes or positions on live Windows, counted at cost, and at nothing once a Window resolves against the vault. |
| Range & Moonshot | The reserve is the counterparty to every band and target ticket. | The locked payout of each ticket until its closing print lands. |
| Parlay | The reserve backs combined Window tickets and keeps the stake when a leg misses. | Each ticket's full payout until its last leg resolves. |
| Boost | The reserve fronts part of each boosted position and takes a premium for it. | Fronted capital until a position closes, knocks out or settles. |

1. Take a seat and select the reserve you mean. Inspect its value, utilization, risk text and active commitments before entering an amount under **Supply**.
2. Review the supply quote before you accept it: the venue issues shares at the reserve's current share price. The position view shows your shares, share price, realized return and current value separately.
3. Use **Withdraw** to take the idle part back at the current share price. Committed capital remains until its Window, round, ticket or Boost resolves. The maker tab can merge paired positions and settle a closed Window before withdrawal.

The maker tab's Windows table shows what the vault holds on each Window, paired sets, one-sided inventory and settlement. The other tabs show their reserve bounds and committed capital. When new deposits are paused, existing positions continue settling and idle withdrawals remain available. Read the **selected tab's** live state before accepting: a past run does not guarantee that quotes are running or that a particular amount is withdrawable.

Each reserve's value is a `NavStatement` the venue publishes on the ledger, and your shares are an `LpShare` contract between your seat and the venue. The maker vault's value moves only when a Window resolves, never at a quote or a merge. Current pool capacity belongs to the live app, not to a dated receipt.
