<AppLink href="/short">Short</AppLink>, under **More → More ways to trade**, uses a Down position held by the Boost reserve. It uses DevNet test credits. Your loss is capped at the stake, but the reserve is repaid before you receive any remainder.

## Open a short

1. Take a funded seat. Choose an asset, Window length and trading Window.
2. Enter a stake and select an offered multiple.
3. Read the premium, position size, possible return and knock-out line.
4. Confirm the venue's quote, then check **Your shorts** for the accepted position.

Stocks follow the venue's session calendar. Use the app's displayed open and close times, including holidays and early closes, rather than a fixed UTC interval. Eligible 24/7 assets can appear separately. A Window listed for later cannot open a short yet; **Schedule a Down call** opens a plain [pre-open call](/trading/pre-open-calls) instead.

## Close or wait

**Close** requests a firm buy-back quote. The reserve takes back what it fronted and returns the remainder to your seat. No venue quote means no honest current mark and no exit.

The knock-out line is fixed at issue. A knock-out needs the Window's oracle quorum at the line; the venue cannot trigger it from an unsupported display price. At the end, settlement follows the Window's Resolution. A void returns the stake. Follow any claim or refund action shown for that position.

This route needs the deployed Boost contracts, reserve capacity and a live quote. A capability message or paused reserve means a new short cannot be opened.

**Hosted check — 8 October 2026:** an AAPL 15-minute short opened with **Short open: 3 contracts of AAPL Down.** Its card showed 0.81 staked. The mark and exit were initially unavailable; a quote and **Close** later appeared, then became unavailable again. No confirmed close was recorded. The successful opening does not establish a completed open-and-close journey or a knock-out.
