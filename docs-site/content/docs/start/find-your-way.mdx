The desktop rail has **six places**, in this order. The phone dock opens Trade, Markets and Portfolio directly, with the remaining destinations in More.

| Key | Place | What you do there |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | <AppLink href="/trade/BTC">Trade</AppLink> | Choose Up or Down on a live price Window. |
| 2 | <AppLink href="/markets">Markets</AppLink> | Browse listed Windows, stocks and events. |
| 3 | <AppLink href="/portfolio">Portfolio</AppLink> | Read your balance, positions and record. |
| 4 | <AppLink href="/games">Games</AppLink> | Open Practice, market games and arcade runs. |
| 5 | <AppLink href="/strategies">Automate</AppLink> | Find strategies, agents and desks. |
| 6 | <AppLink href="/leaderboard">Leaderboard</AppLink> | Compare published records. |

Number keys navigate when you are not typing into a field. **⌘K** on Mac, or **Ctrl+K** elsewhere, opens page search. Open **More** for the full directory.

## Your seat and theme

Choose **Take a seat**, then **Guest Seat**, to start. Once leased, open the seat avatar for the party id, copy control, **Use on another device** and reset. The rail's **+** opens Add funds. [Your seat](/start/wallet) explains linking; [Balances](/trading/balances) explains test funds.

Use the sun/moon control to change the theme. Read the environment label beside your seat: **Test funds · DevNet** describes this hosted venue. The sky Guest demo has paper credits; the pink DevNet mode has a leased seat and test funds. The guest-seat avatar uses lime. Light/dark changes the theme within that environment. Neither balance has cash value.
