The [five baskets](/trading/baskets) each offer Predict and Automate. Automate opens the [desk studio](/agents/desk), where anyone can draft without a seat. For the current state of each lane and what it needs, see [availability](/help/availability).

## Choose light or dark

Use the sun/moon control in the header. Your choice is saved on this device; the app follows your system preference until you choose one.
