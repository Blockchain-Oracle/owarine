---
title: "Make your first call"
description: "Go from browsing to a settled result with demo credits."
---

A call is a prediction about a listed price during a **Window**: one asset, one open, one close, one cadence. Choose Up or Down, pay the amount shown, then wait for the closing print to settle it.

Agari runs on a **Canton test network**. The money is **demo credits**, worth nothing anywhere else. You can browse <AppLink href="/markets">Markets</AppLink> and read every price without a seat.

<Steps>
<Step>
### Take a seat

Open <AppLink href="/markets">Markets</AppLink> and choose **Take a seat**. This browser makes a signing key that cannot leave it, and the venue leases you a Canton party of your own. There is no wallet to install and no network to switch. [Your seat](/start/wallet) explains what that means.

Taking a seat does not place a call.
</Step>
<Step>
### Your demo credits

A new seat starts with demo credits. Canton charges no network fee, so there is nothing else to top up.
</Step>
<Step>
### Choose a Window and a side

Pick a ticker and a live Window. **Up** wins when the closing print is at or above the opening print; **Down** wins otherwise (a tie goes to Up).
</Step>
<Step>
### Review and place

Enter your stake and side. The venue gives you a **firm quote**: one price, valid for a few seconds. The ticket shows the exact cost, fee included, before you accept. Accepting creates your position, which only your seat and the venue can see.
</Step>
<Step>
### Watch and collect

Open <AppLink href="/portfolio">Portfolio</AppLink> to watch the Window. When it closes, the resolver settles it from the oracle parties' signed prints — or voids it if too few prints arrived or they disagreed too much, and a void returns your stake and your fee. The venue pays settled positions to your seat; each result's evidence opens on the proof page.
</Step>
</Steps>

[Your first call, in full](/trading/first-trade) covers what a void looks like. [What is available](/help/availability) explains which parts of the app are running now and which are planned.
