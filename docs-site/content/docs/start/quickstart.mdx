---
title: "Make your first call"
description: "Take a seat, get demo credits, and go from browsing to a settled result."
---

A call is a prediction about a listed price during a **Window**: one asset, one open, one close, one cadence. Choose Up or Down, accept the price shown, then wait for the closing print to settle it.

Owarine runs on **Canton**. The money is **demo credits**, worth nothing anywhere else. You can browse <AppLink href="/markets">Markets</AppLink> and read every price without a seat. Today the whole path below runs on a local Canton sandbox; there is no hosted URL yet.

<Steps>
<Step>
### Take a seat

Open <AppLink href="/markets">Markets</AppLink> and choose **Take a seat**. This browser makes an ed25519 signing key that cannot be exported, and the venue leases you a Canton party of your own. There is no wallet to install and no network to switch. [Your seat](/start/wallet) explains what that means.

Taking a seat does not place a call.

<GuideCapture name="markets" caption="A live ETH 1-minute Window before a seat is taken: the opening print is the line, and the ticket asks you to take a seat before it quotes." />
</Step>
<Step>
### Your demo credits

The venue credits a new seat with 1,000 demo credits, once per lease. Canton charges no network fee, so there is nothing else to top up.
</Step>
<Step>
### Choose a Window and a side

Pick a ticker and a live Window. **Up** wins when the closing print is at or above the opening print; **Down** wins otherwise (a tie goes to Up).
</Step>
<Step>
### Review and place

Enter your stake and side. The venue gives you a **firm quote** from its published price ladder: one price, held for about 20 seconds and only for your seat. The ticket shows the exact cost, fee included, before you accept. Accepting creates your position, which only your seat and the venue can see; the ticket names the ledger update it landed in.
</Step>
<Step>
### Watch and collect

Open <AppLink href="/portfolio">Portfolio</AppLink> to watch the Window. When it closes, the resolver and the venue record the result from the oracle parties' prints, or void it if too few prints arrived or they disagreed by more than 1%; a void returns your stake and your fee. The venue's settler pays your seat without you signing anything, and each result opens its proof page.
</Step>
</Steps>

[Your first call, in full](/trading/first-trade) covers what a void looks like. [What is available](/help/availability) explains which parts of the app run now, and what each one needs.
