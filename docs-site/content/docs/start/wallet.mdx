<Walkthrough name="take-a-seat" />

Open <AppLink href="/markets">Markets</AppLink>, choose **Take a seat**, then **Guest Seat**. The browser creates a signing key and asks the venue to lease a Canton party. No extension, seed phrase or account is needed.

A successful lease puts your seat's avatar in the rail. Open it to see the party id, **Copy party id**, the lease countdown and **Test funds · DevNet**. A key without a leased party cannot trade; the menu offers **Lease a party** in that state.

## Guest demo and a DevNet seat

| State | What you use | Where positions live |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Browsing Trade without a seat | 10,000 paper credits | This browser; no Canton position |
| A leased guest seat | DevNet test credits | Contracts on Canton DevNet |
| A practice desk | Its own paper balance | The desk's practice record |

The Trade screen uses your seat after you connect. Paper credits do not turn into DevNet credits. Every balance here is for testing and has no cash value. See [Balances](/trading/balances) for funding and pockets.

## What the browser signs

The browser keeps a non-exportable ed25519 key in its local storage database. It signs requests that prove which seat is asking; the server submits the corresponding Canton commands. Your cash and ordinary positions name your party and the venue as stakeholders. The venue is still the counterparty and operates the demo parties' ledger access. Read [the trust boundary](/architecture/overview) before treating this as independent wallet custody.

## Use on another device

<Walkthrough name="another-device" />

1. Open the seat menu and choose **Use on another device**.
2. Scan the QR code or enter the eight-character code at <AppLink href="/seat/link">Seat link</AppLink> on your other device.
3. On the first device, check the key shown under **Allow this device?**, then choose **Allow**. Choose **Not mine** for a request you did not start.
4. Wait for **Linked** before using the second device.

Codes expire after 60 seconds and work once. Showing a QR is only the first step; it does not mean the second device has joined.

**Hosted check — 8 October 2026:** the seat menu opened the QR, manual code, expiry and Copy control. No second device joined or was approved in this check.

## Lease and reset

Seats come from a shared pool. The menu's countdown shows your current lease, which renews while you use it. If the pool is full, the page shows your place in line and waits for a free seat.

**Reset seat** deletes this browser's key. A holder reset releases the lease for cleanup; a joined device reset removes that device. Cleanup closes calls on still-trading Windows at their cost, including the fee, lets decided calls settle, and sweeps the remaining test credits before the party returns to the pool. The next visitor's history starts with their own lease.

Check <AppLink href="/portfolio">Portfolio</AppLink> after an uncertain trade result before trying again. [Status](/help/status) shows whether the venue and seat pool are answering.
