description: "Take a seat, get demo credits, and what happens when a seat is released or reset."

Agari has no wallet to connect. Choose **Take a seat** in the header and you are ready to call: there is no extension to install, no network to switch and no fee to pay.

## What a seat is

Taking a seat does two things:

- **This browser makes a signing key** that cannot be exported — the page itself cannot read its private half. Every call you place is signed with it.
- **The venue leases you a Canton party of your own.** Your cash and your positions are contracts that only your seat's party and the venue can see. Another visitor cannot, and neither can the oracles.

The seat is leased only when you click; browsing <AppLink href="/markets">Markets</AppLink> and <AppLink href="/proof">Proof</AppLink> needs no seat.

## Demo credits

A new seat is funded with **demo credits**, the venue's test money on a Canton test network. They size every call and ticket, and they are worth nothing anywhere else; there is no way to move them off the venue. Canton charges no network fee, so there is nothing else to top up.

## When a seat is released

Seats come from a small shared pool. A seat you have left idle, with no open positions, goes back to the pool. Before it is reused, its open quotes are withdrawn, any open positions close at what they cost, and its cash is swept, so the next visitor starts from an empty party and never sees yours. A seat with open positions is not released before they settle. If every seat is taken, the page says when the next one frees and takes it for you.

## Resetting a seat

**Reset seat** in the seat menu lets the seat go and deletes this browser's key. The key cannot be recovered, and a new seat starts from fresh demo credits.

## If a call does not go through

A signed call is not a filled one. If the ticket does not show a result, check <AppLink href="/portfolio">Portfolio</AppLink> before trying again, and <AppLink href="/status">Status</AppLink> for the venue's health. Each settled call's evidence opens on the proof page.
