The web app works on a phone: open it in Safari or Chrome and add it to your Home Screen. It is the same product as on a desktop, on the same venue, with the same seat and demo credits. Today it runs against a local Canton sandbox; there is no hosted URL to open yet.

## The iPhone app (built, not on TestFlight yet)

A native iPhone app is built for Canton: it has its own identity, typechecks with every change and its iOS build exports cleanly. It is **not on TestFlight yet**. Its App Store Connect record does not exist yet, and the app has not yet been run on a phone or simulator against a Canton venue. There is no Android APK. Earlier builds of the app do not work with this venue. When the TestFlight invitation exists, the app's <AppLink href="/download">Download</AppLink> page shows it with a QR code, and an Android APK appears there the same way, with its SHA-256 to check it; until then each card says what it waits on.

## Your seat on a phone

There is no wallet app to open. **Take a seat** works as on the web: the phone makes an ed25519 signing key and keeps it in the iPhone Keychain, for this device only (never synced or restored onto another phone), and the venue leases a Canton party for it. The first-run pages end with the demo-credits terms, and accepting them takes the seat. To use a seat you already hold on the web, choose **Have a seat on the web? Link this phone** and enter or scan the code from **Use on another device**. [Your seat](/start/wallet) explains both. There is no network fee.

## What the native app does differently

- **A first-run walkthrough.** Short screens explain a Window, how it settles and demo credits.
- **Bottom drawers.** The Up/Down ticket and your account rise from the bottom of the screen, and the ticket shows its receipt in place.
- **One tap.** The ticket's chip reads **one tap**: a seat already places a call with one tap, so there is no second key to arm.
- **Who can see this.** The same chip and per-party view as the web show that only your seat and the venue can see your positions.
- **Pull to refresh** on every page.
