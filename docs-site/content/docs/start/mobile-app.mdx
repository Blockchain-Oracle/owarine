The web app works on a phone: open it in Safari or Chrome and add it to your Home Screen. It is the same product as on a desktop, on the same venue, with the same seat and demo credits. The public web app is at `https://owarine.xyz`; read its current network and service state before trading.

## The native iPhone app

The native client targets Canton and has its own device identity. Earlier local-stack iOS Simulator journeys are recorded: first run, seats, calls, settlement, privacy views and linking. Physical-device acceptance, a public TestFlight invitation, Android distribution and real push delivery were not verified in this 8 October documentation pass. Use the app's <AppLink href="/download">Download</AppLink> page for current distribution links and their stated conditions. A past build or simulator run does not establish a public invitation.

## Your seat on a phone

There is no wallet app to open. **Take a seat** works as on the web: the phone makes an ed25519 signing key and keeps it in the iPhone Keychain, for this device only (never synced or restored onto another phone), and the venue leases a Canton party for it. The first-run pages end with the demo-credits terms, and accepting them takes the seat. To use a seat you already hold on the web, choose **Have a seat on the web? Link this phone** and enter or scan the code from **Use on another device**. [Your seat](/start/wallet) explains both.

## What the native app does differently

- **A first-run walkthrough.** Short screens explain a Window, how it settles and demo credits.
- **Bottom drawers.** The Up/Down ticket and your account rise from the bottom of the screen, and the ticket shows its receipt in place.
- **One tap.** The ticket's chip reads **one tap**: a seat already places a call with one tap, so there is no second key to arm.
- **Who can see this.** The same chip as the web shows that only your seat and the venue can see your positions.
- **Pull to refresh** on every page.
