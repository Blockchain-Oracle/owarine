Agari runs on a phone in the browser today: open the site in Safari or Chrome and add it to your Home Screen. It is the same product as the website, on the same Canton test network, with the same demo credits.

## The native app (planned)

A native app for iPhone and Android is being ported to Canton. It will be offered through a public TestFlight link and a downloadable APK once it is ready; until then there is no Canton build to install, and earlier builds of the app do not work with this venue.

## Your seat on a phone

There is no wallet app to open. **Take a seat** works the same way as on the web: the phone makes a signing key and keeps it in its secure storage, for this device only (never synced or restored onto another phone), and the venue leases a Canton party for it. [Your seat](/start/wallet) explains what that means. There is no network fee.

## What the native app does differently

- **A first-run walkthrough.** Short screens explain a Window, how it settles and demo credits.
- **Bottom drawers.** The Up/Down ticket and your account rise from the bottom of the screen, and the ticket shows its receipt in place.
- **One tap.** A seat already places a call with one tap; there is no separate session key to arm.
- **Pull to refresh** on every page.
