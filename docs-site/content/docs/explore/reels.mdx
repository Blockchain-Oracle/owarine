---
title: "Reels and takes"
description: "Short, seat-signed calls on a live Window, woven into a feed."
---

A **take** is a short, public call on a live Window — a side and an optional line of why, signed with your seat's key (an ed25519 message signature, not a ledger transaction) and verified on the server before it's shown. Reels is the feed those takes flow into, woven between the live Windows, with your desk's latest decision when you have one.

## Why a signature, not just a post

The signature ties a take to the seat that made it, the same way a call is tied to the seat that placed it. It costs nothing — it's a message signature, not a ledger transaction — but it means a take can't be spoofed as coming from an address that never signed it. Its **backed** badge needs a published call on that Window, so a private position is never revealed.

## Cashtags

A take carries the same asset-mark chip and `$TICKER` cashtag grammar as the news feed, so a call about TSLA shows TSLA's mark right on the card, and choosing the cashtag opens that ticker's hub.

Where Owarine's social store isn't configured, the composer says "Takes aren't connected yet" and the reel still carries every live Window. Takes have not yet been run end to end on a Canton sandbox.

Not investment advice: a take is one person's public call, not a recommendation, and Owarine doesn't rank or promote any take as more credible than another.
