## The Room

Each Window (and each ticker) has a Room, but only seats that have bet in it can speak. The server checks two things before a seat may read or post: the seat signs once to prove the key is its own, and its party has a fill on this Window (or, for a ticker's Room, on any of that ticker's Windows) made while this seat held the lease. A recycled party therefore never lets a later visitor into an earlier one's Room, and a device linked to your seat joins your lease's Rooms and no other. A seat with no position sees the Room as locked, not hidden: it can see that a Room exists, just not post into it.

On a local Canton sandbox, a seat placed a call from the ticket, joined that Window's Room and posted a message. Nothing is on a shared Canton network yet, and where the social store isn't configured the Room says it isn't connected.

## Price alerts

An alert watches a ticker's live price while your browser tab is open — it's a client-side watcher, not a server-side push. It fires only on a fresh tick (published within the last minute) inside today's regular session, so a stale or after-hours print never triggers a rule. When it fires, you get an in-app toast, and — if you've granted the browser's notification permission — a system notification too.

Because the watch runs in your browser, an alert doesn't fire while the tab is closed. If you need to know a stale reading exists at all, <AppLink href="/status">Status</AppLink> and the market's own "last close · as of …" label are the honest always-on signal; alerts are a convenience on top of an open tab, not a background service.
