## Rooms

A Window's Room is for seats that traded it. A ticker Room admits seats that traded one of that ticker's Windows. Take a seat, open the Room, then choose **Join the room** when eligible. Your seat signs a message; the server checks the signature and the trading record before admitting you.

Selling out or settlement does not by itself remove a bettor's eligibility. A missing position leaves the Room locked. A failed gate read asks you to retry rather than pretending no position exists. Rooms also need the social store; **The Room isn't connected yet** means commenting is unavailable on that deployment.

## Price alerts

Create an alert for a ticker and keep the browser tab open. The watcher evaluates fresh ticks. Regular stock alerts wait for the regular session; crypto, pre-IPO and other supported 24/7 names use their round-the-clock price basis.

A trigger shows an in-app toast. With browser notification permission it can also show a system notification. **Closing the tab stops the watcher.** These alerts are not a server push service and do not place trades.

An old or unreadable tick does not trigger a fresh-price alert. Read the asset's as-of time and [Status](/help/status) if its source appears stuck.
