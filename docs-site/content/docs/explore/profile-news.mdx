title: "News and trader profiles"
description: "Read a headline's source and ticker, then separate public trading records from private positions."
---

Open <AppLink href="/news">News</AppLink> under **More → Learn**. Rows show a source, age, sentiment label and any recognized `$TICKER` chips. Choosing a ticker opens its hub with the price and available Windows.

Headlines require the configured news provider. An empty feed or failed read is not a fresh headline. Sentiment is a keyword reading of the headline; it does not decide a Window's result. The signed boundary prints do that.

**Crowd flow** is a different measure: the Up share of calls people opted to publish in the recent window, with a minimum participation threshold. Private calls are not included.

## A public profile

Open a trader from <AppLink href="/leaderboard">Leaderboard</AppLink> to see their profile at `/u/<address>`. Its record and open calls come from what that seat chose to publish. Publication is an explicit action; keeping a position private does not turn it into public profile data.

Treat a published record as a record of those calls, not all the trader's activity or a guarantee of future results. **Copy this trader**, where offered, opens a mirror-strategy draft; it does not fund a permission until you review and authorize it. See [Copy a strategy](/agents/copy-strategies).
