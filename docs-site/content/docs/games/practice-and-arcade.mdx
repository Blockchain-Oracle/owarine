title: "Practice and arcade"
description: "Play real-price prompts or seeded score runs without placing a position."
---

<AppLink href="/games/practice">Practice</AppLink> deals the venue's live Windows as swipe cards. Swipe up or down, use arrow keys, or tap a button. After the final swipe it watches the live price feed for a short fixed interval and scores every card at the same moment. The opponent is a labelled coin flip. **The score is not the Window's eventual settlement.** There is no stake, position, payout, ledger transaction or rating, and nothing is signed. If the live Window list or price feed is unavailable, no round can be scored. Out of stock hours Practice deals only from the Windows that trade around the clock, and says so when none has enough time left for a round.

<AppLink href="/games/line-rider">Line Rider</AppLink> and <AppLink href="/games/candle-hop">Candle Hop</AppLink> are arcade runs. Line Rider asks you to keep a dot on a scrolling line; Candle Hop asks you to pass through candle gaps. A visible seed generates the course. Inputs are recorded and the server replays a completed run before posting its score. Posting to a board needs your seat's proof, so only the seat a score names can post it; a deployment without the games store keeps the score only on your screen.

Arcade scores are **server checked, not on the ledger**, and no money rides on them. Practice and the arcade are read-only or off-ledger by design, and neither has been part of a recorded run against the local Canton sandbox yet. [Duel](/games/duel-and-lucky) is the money-backed game.
