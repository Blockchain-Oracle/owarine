<AppLink href="/games">Games</AppLink> is one of the rail's six places. Open a mode to read its current availability, cost and result rule.

| Mode | What you do | Money and proof |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Practice](/games/practice-and-arcade) | Call Up or Down on a dealt deck, then watch a short price interval | No stake; score is not Window settlement |
| [Duel](/games/duel-and-lucky) | Play another seat on a committed deck | Picks spend test credits; Ranked can add a side-pot |
| [Lucky](/games/duel-and-lucky) | Spin to find a possible call, then review it | Spin alone spends nothing; an accepted call uses test credits |
| [Range](/trading/range-and-moonshot) | Pick inside or outside a band | Reserve-backed DevNet round |
| [Moonshot](/trading/range-and-moonshot) | Aim at a price level | Reserve-backed DevNet round |
| [Line Rider and Candle Hop](/games/practice-and-arcade) | Play a seeded arcade course | No stake; server-checked score, off ledger |

Window games need eligible markets and quotes. Duel also needs the room service, matchmaker and another player. A closed stock session can leave only eligible 24/7 Windows; a 24/7 label does not guarantee a current quote. Arcade courses do not depend on a Window's settlement.

[Status](/help/status) helps separate an unavailable source or service from a round that is still waiting for its result.
