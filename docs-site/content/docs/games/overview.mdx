<AppLink href="/games">Games</AppLink> has seven modes: Practice, Duel, Lucky, Range, Moonshot, Line Rider and Candle Hop. They share Agari's Windows, but have different scoring and money rules. A particular round still needs its own current game state, quotes or participants.

Start with [Practice and arcade](/games/practice-and-arcade) to play without a stake. [Duel and Lucky](/games/duel-and-lucky) place real calls from your seat. [Range and Moonshot](/trading/range-and-moonshot) use a separate prefunded reserve. Read the entry cost and result rule in each mode before placing anything; money-backed games use **demo credits**, pay straight into your seat's cash, and are separate from the [desk](/agents/desk). The Duel, its rank and season pool, Range and Moonshot have been played end to end on a local Canton sandbox; nothing in Games is on a hosted test network yet.

While the stock market is closed, every mode that plays a Window is marked **24/7 only** with the session (for example "Market closed · Opens Wed 09:30 ET · pre-IPO and baskets only"): it runs on the round-the-clock Windows alone. The arcade runs never touch a Window and are unaffected.

The [ordinary call guide](/trading/first-trade) explains the Window beneath the games. Use <AppLink href="/status">Status</AppLink> if a round is not listing or settling.
