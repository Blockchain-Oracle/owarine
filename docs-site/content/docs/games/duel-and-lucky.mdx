## Duel

At <AppLink href="/games/duel">Duel</AppLink>, two players receive the same committed deck of live Windows. Choose Free or Ranked, inspect the per-card cap and any side-pot, then find an opponent. Free means **no side-pot**; every pick is still a real call using your demo credits. Ranked adds a demo-credit side-pot and a rating. Each pick is a call your seat places with no prompt and no network fee. Each player keeps what their own positions pay, while the pot follows the match result or forfeit rules and lands straight in cash: there is nothing to claim.

A card is dealt only from a Window whose ladder is quoting both Up and Down, never from a 5-minute Window, and never from a Window past its series' close (a stock series ends at the session close; a Gap Window has no successor). Out of hours that leaves the round-the-clock Windows with live quotes; when none has one, the queue says so instead of dealing a deck that cannot fill.

The deck's hash goes on the ledger when the match opens, before either player sees a card, and `Duel_Reveal` recomputes its SHA-256 and refuses a deck that does not match. After the cards' Windows resolve, the match is scored and finalized on the ledger: the winner takes both pots, a tie splits them, and a player who does not finish forfeits. If neither finishes, or the deck is never revealed, both pots are returned and the result says why. The venue's settler normally sends these steps, but either player may. A usable duel requires the arena contract, the room server, the matchmaker, another player and dealable Windows; the route reports which one is missing. A queued match or unsettled card is not a paid result.

A Ranked result moves both ratings, and <AppLink href="/games/rank">Rank</AppLink> lists the ratings that verified duels produced. A season pool pays its winners once, after the season ends, and only to a seat that still holds its lease. A decided Ranked duel and a season payout have run on a local Canton sandbox; the Duel is not on a hosted test network yet.

## Lucky

At <AppLink href="/games/lucky">Lucky</AppLink>, enter a stake and spin. A server seed is committed before the reels move; your browser adds its seed. The draw names an asset, side and reach, then shows a **live quote** on a live 15-minute or 1-hour Window with at least two minutes left. You decide whether to place one ordinary call from your seat. If the ladder moves, the live quote may differ from the reel's reach; review that difference before accepting. It settles like any other venue position.

What a spin can draw depends on the hour. While any stock Window trades, the list is the nine launch stocks plus the 24/7 lanes (OpenAI and the five baskets). While none trades, it is the 24/7 lanes alone, so an out-of-hours spin never draws a stock with nothing to deal. The list is sealed with the server seed as a numbered policy, so a draw can be replayed against the exact list it was made from.

Lucky's browser check proves the revealed seed matches the commitment and replays the draw from the seeds. The candidate Window set and its selection rule are recorded, but that check does not prove the server chose the candidate set fairly. Neither a spin nor a deal spends credits until a call is placed.

On the local sandbox a call was placed from Lucky's own screen: a QQQ Up draw at 2× reach, taken at the venue's firm quote as one ordinary call that settles with its Window. Spins made at night, when only the 24/7 lanes trade and PreStocks was rate-limiting the host, found no quotable Window and answered **No deal** instead. For the underlying order and settlement rules, see [Place a trade](/trading/first-trade).
