## More resources

Owarine is a prediction market on Canton Network. You can browse <AppLink href="/markets">Markets</AppLink> and <AppLink href="/proof">Proof</AppLink> without a seat. **Predict** uses demo credits, and each position is visible only to your seat and the venue. The first-call journey has recorded DevNet acceptance from 6 October 2026, and the Canton Coin rail from 7 October. Broader features have separate local evidence; do not infer their network readiness from the site being online. The public app responded over HTTPS on 8 October. Start with [your seat and demo credits](/start/wallet) for a call, or [the price sources](/architecture/price-sources) to see how a Window settles.
