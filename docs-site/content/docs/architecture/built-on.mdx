Agari is built on three platforms. Each row below says what that platform does for the product **today**, and where the evidence is. Everything described runs on a local Canton sandbox; nothing is on DevNet or a hosted URL yet.

## Why Canton

A prediction market's positions are the information a competitor wants. On a public chain, owner, side and size are readable in the block that records them. On Canton a contract goes only to its stakeholders' nodes: a `Leg` has two signatories, the owner and the venue, and an outsider's ledger query comes back empty. The app runs that query live in "Who can see this", with the request body on screen, and `Test.Privacy.testOutsiderSeesNothing` proves it in Daml. <AppLink href="/who-sees-what">Who sees what</AppLink> lists every contract and the parties that receive it.

## Why governed resolution

The only irreversible act in a prediction market is settlement. The venue already cannot resolve a Window (`Test.Resolution.testVenueCannotResolve`), but the resolver is still one party with one key. With BitSafe's governance engine the resolver becomes a committee: a resolve names the expected side, needs two confirmations, executes exactly once, and fails if the evidence says otherwise. `abu-pm-main` itself does not change.
