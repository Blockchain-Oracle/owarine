Owarine is built on two platforms. Each row below says what that platform does for the product and where the recorded evidence is.

| Platform | What it does for Owarine | State |

## Why Canton

A prediction market's positions are the information a competitor wants. On a public chain, owner, side and size are readable in the block that records them. On Canton a contract goes only to its stakeholders' nodes: a `Leg` has two signatories, the owner and the venue, and an outsider's ledger query comes back empty. The app says so on every position with the **Who can see this** chip, and `Test.Privacy.testOutsiderSeesNothing` proves it in Daml.
