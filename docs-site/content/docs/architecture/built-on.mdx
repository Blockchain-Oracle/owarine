Owarine is built on two platforms. Each row below says what that platform does for the product **today**, and where the evidence is.

| Platform | What it does for Owarine | State |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Canton Network** | The ledger. Every position is a `Leg` contract signed by its owner and the venue, so no other party's node receives it. The money rules, quotes, prints, resolution and settlement are five Daml packages written for this venue ([The Daml packages](/architecture/programs)). | Uploaded and vetted on the Noders DevNet node |
| **Noders** | Noders organises HackCanton Season 3 on its AppsFactory platform and runs the shared DevNet node (Canton 3.5.19). Parties on that node are created in the Noders Console, and every upload is made there. | The five packages are vetted there and the first call settled on 6 Oct |

## Why Canton

A prediction market's positions are the information a competitor wants. On a public chain, owner, side and size are readable in the block that records them. On Canton a contract goes only to its stakeholders' nodes: a `Leg` has two signatories, the owner and the venue, and an outsider's ledger query comes back empty. The app runs that query live in "Who can see this", with the request body on screen, and `Test.Privacy.testOutsiderSeesNothing` proves it in Daml. <AppLink href="/who-sees-what">Who sees what</AppLink> lists every contract and the parties that receive it.
