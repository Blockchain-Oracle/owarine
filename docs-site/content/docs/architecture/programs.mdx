Agari's rules are one Daml package, `abu-pm-main` (Daml SDK 3.5), with its tests in `pm-tests`. Every contract names the parties that sign it and the parties that may see it; the ledger enforces both.

| Contract | Signed by · seen by | What it holds |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `Series` | venue · resolver, auditor | A lane's cadence, the oracle list, the quorum, the deviation limit and its append-only price-policy versions. `Series_OpenWindow` opens the next Window. |
| `MarketTerms` | venue · resolver | One Window's immutable terms: trading start, lock, expiry, deadlines, and the oracle list and quorum copied from its Series. |
| `WindowState` → `OpenPrint` → `Resolution` | resolver and venue | The single-use resolution chain. Each step consumes the one before, so a Window resolves or voids **exactly once**. |
| `PriceQuote` | an oracle · venue, resolver | One oracle's close price for a symbol at a boundary, with a hash of the raw source payload. |
| `VenueDesk`, `Quote` | venue · the quoted seat | The venue's firm offer for one side of one Window at one price, valid until a set time. |
| `Leg` | venue and owner | A position. Accepting a quote creates a pair: your leg and the venue's opposite leg. Your fee is held in your leg. |
| `VenueAccount`, `VenueCash` | venue and owner | A seat's account and its demo credits. |
| `Publication` | venue and owner | An opt-in public record of a call, used by leaderboards and takes. |

## How a call works

1. The venue's roller opens a Window with `Series_OpenWindow`.
2. Your seat asks for a quote; the venue's desk issues a `Quote` (`Desk_IssueQuote`) at a price on its published ladder, locking its own stake.
3. You accept it with `Quote_Accept`. Your cash must cover the stake plus the fee. Two `Leg`s are created; only you and the venue can see yours.
4. The resolver records the opening print (`Terms_RecordOpen`) and, after the close, resolves the Window (`Terms_Resolve`) from the oracle quotes, or voids it (`Terms_Void`) if the quorum is missing or the oracles disagree too much.
5. The venue's settler pays the Window's legs in batches with `Desk_SettleBatch`. A won leg pays its full quantity; a void returns stake plus fee. If the venue never settles, you can take your stake back yourself after the refund time (`Leg_RefundStale`).

## What the tests prove

The `pm-tests` package checks, among others: money is conserved after every step of seeded random sequences; the venue cannot resolve a Window, confiscate a position or accept a quote for you; the resolver cannot resolve without the oracle quorum; a Window resolves or voids exactly once; an outsider sees nothing and a user sees only their own positions. No external audit is claimed.
