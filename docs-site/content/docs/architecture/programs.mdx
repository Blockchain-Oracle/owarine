Agari's rules are five Daml packages released together as R1 (Daml SDK 3.5), plus a sixth for governed resolution that runs on LocalNet only, all tested by the `pm-tests` Daml Script suite (273 scripts pass, the Canton Coin rail's and governance's included). Every contract names the parties that sign it and the parties that may see it; the ledger enforces both. The release files, with package ids and hashes, are in `daml/released/`, and `dpm upgrade-check` passes from the previous versions.

| Package | Version | What it holds |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `abu-pm-main` | 0.5.2 | Windows, prints, quotes, positions, cash, resolution, events, grants, reserves, the maker vault and pre-open resting calls. The others depend on it. |
| `abu-pm-tickets` | 0.1.4 | Range and Moonshot, Parlay, Boost (a short is Boost on Down) and Earn. |
| `abu-pm-agents` | 0.2.2 | Strategies, subscriptions, creator fees, grant top-ups and the desk mandate. |
| `abu-pm-games` | 0.1.2 | Duels, their results and the season prize pool. |
| `abu-pm-cc` | 0.1.1 | The Canton Coin rail on the CIP-56 token standard: a deposit credits exactly the coin received, a withdrawal is a token-standard transfer back, and an auditor sees the coin held against the coin owed. Proved in Daml Script and unit tests; not run on a network yet, and off in code until DevNet proves it. |
| `abu-pm-governance` | 0.1.0 | Governed resolution: the resolver becomes a committee on BitSafe's released `GovernanceRules`, so a print, a resolve or a void needs two of three confirmations. LocalNet only, never part of R1. See [Built on](/architecture/built-on). |

## The core contracts

| Contract | Signed by · seen by | What it holds |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `Series` | venue · resolver, auditor | A lane's cadence, oracle list, quorum of 2, 1% deviation limit and append-only price-policy versions. |
| `MarketTerms` | venue · resolver | One Window's fixed terms: start, lock, expiry, deadlines, refund time. |
| `PriceQuote` | an oracle · venue, resolver | One oracle's price for a symbol at a boundary, with the sha-256 of the raw source payload. |
| `OpenPrint` → `Resolution` | resolver and venue | The single-use resolution chain: each step consumes the one before, so a Window resolves or voids **exactly once**. |
| `Quote`, `BuyQuote` | venue · the quoted seat | The venue's firm offer to sell you a side, or buy one back, at one price until a set time. |
| `Leg` | venue and owner | A position. Accepting a quote creates a pair: your leg and the venue's opposite one. A private call's leg pays back into your private balance, win, loss or void. |
| `VenueAccount`, `VenueCash` | venue and owner | A seat's account and its demo credits. |
| `RestingDesk` → `RestingOffer` → `RestingCall` | venue, then venue and owner | A pre-open call: the venue's offer to hold it (before the bell only), then your stake held inside a call only you and the venue see. |
| `Publication`, `SettlementReceipt` | venue and owner | An opt-in public record of a call, and the receipt a finished position leaves (it names the private balance when a private call paid back into it). |
| `EventTerms` → `EventVerdict` | venue, then resolver and venue | A committee event and its one verdict. |

## How a call works

1. The roller opens a Window with `Series_OpenWindow` (a Monday Gap with `Series_OpenWindowSpan`, an event with `Series_OpenEvent`).
2. Your seat asks for a quote; the venue issues a `Quote` with `Desk_IssueQuote` at a price on its published ladder, locking its own side.
3. You accept it with `Quote_Accept`. Your cash must cover the stake plus the fee. Two `Leg`s are created; only you and the venue can see yours.
4. The resolver records the open (`Terms_RecordOpen`), then resolves (`Terms_Resolve`) from the oracle quotes, or voids (`Terms_Void`) if the quorum is missing or the quotes disagree.
5. The settler pays the Window's legs in batches with `Desk_SettleBatch`; you sign nothing. A won leg pays its full quantity; a void returns stake plus fee.
6. If the venue never settles, you can claim against the resolution yourself (`Leg_Claim`), or from the refund time take back stake and fee with `Leg_RefundStale`, which needs no operator.

A call rested before the bell is different: the venue makes an offer (`RestDesk_Offer`), you place it with your own cash (`RestOffer_Place`), and after the bell the venue fills it at exactly your price (`Rest_Fill`), in whole or in part, with the same two `Leg`s. You can cancel it any time (`Rest_Cancel`), and the venue returns an unfilled one at its expiry (`Rest_Expire`).

Selling early is a `BuyQuote` you accept (`BuyQuote_Accept`). Draining a released seat closes its legs at cost with `Leg_CloseOut`, which needs both the venue and the seat.

## What the tests prove

`pm-tests` checks, among others: money is conserved after every step of seeded random sequences; the venue cannot resolve a Window, confiscate a position or accept a quote for you; the resolver cannot resolve without the oracle quorum; a Window resolves or voids exactly once, and settle and stale refund never both run; an outsider sees nothing; tickets, grants, the desk, duels and the maker vault each keep their conservation rules. No external audit is claimed.
