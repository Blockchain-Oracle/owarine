The desk spans the browser, an off-chain runner and, when it goes live, a Daml mandate on Canton. The owner picks the basket and the limits. The runner reads prices and proposes a buy, a sell or a checkpoint. Every decision is written down as a `desk.v1` record and fingerprinted before anything moves.

| Part | Authority |
| --- | --- |
| Owner (your seat) | Picks the basket and limits; approves practice decisions by signing a message. Only the owner could ever withdraw. |
| Desk runner | Chooses timing and proposes allowed actions. It cannot change the owner's limits or withdraw. |
| Price reference | The oracle parties' quorum price, the same signed prints that settle Windows. |

## Practice desks today

A practice desk is a **paper ledger**: the same decision pipeline on real prices, with a paper cash balance and no ledger transaction. **Check it** recomputes a record's fingerprint and compares it with the stored one. A practice desk has no ledger seal, so the check can only compare its paper record.

## The live leg (planned)

On Canton the live desk is planned as a **mandate built on an agent grant**. `AgentGrant` is already in the engine: an owner moves a budget into a grant, and the agent may trade only within its caps — price, per trade, per day, budget and open positions — checked inside the ledger choice, not by the runner. A desk mandate would add the basket's allowed markets, a premium ceiling, pause and unpause by the owner, and a hash-chained checkpoint contract for every decision, so a record could be checked against the ledger.

The live leg would trade **this venue's own markets** with venue cash, and only once the Canton Coin rail lands. There is no external exchange route and no real-money desk on Canton yet; every desk screen that mentions going live says so.
