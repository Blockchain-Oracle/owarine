Agari is a prediction market on **Canton Network** for short price calls: will a price close its Window up or down. The money rules live in **Daml packages**, not in the web app. The venue quotes a firm price for each side; you accept from your seat; three oracle parties sign the prices; a resolver party resolves each Window exactly once; the venue's settler pays. It all runs end to end on a **local Canton sandbox** with **demo credits**; nothing is on DevNet or a hosted URL yet.

## Who can see what

Canton shows a contract only to its stakeholders. Your position (a `Leg`) and your cash (`VenueCash`) each have two: **the venue and your seat's party**. Another visitor, an oracle and the resolver see nothing of yours.

The app shows the rule rather than asserting it. The **Who can see this** chip sits on the held price and each position, and the view switcher on a market page runs the same live query as you, Alice, Bob and an outsider, printing the literal query it sent. In the local rehearsal the outsider got 0 rows while a seat held its position; the venue, as counterparty, saw both seats' legs.

## Seats instead of wallets

There is no wallet app to install. **Take a seat** makes a signing key in your browser that cannot leave it, and the venue leases you a Canton party of your own, funded with 1,000 demo credits. An idle seat with nothing open is released; before reuse its positions close at cost and its cash is swept, so the next visitor starts empty. There is no network fee. [Your seat](/start/wallet) has the details.

## The trust boundary

Every party Agari uses (venue, resolver, oracles, seats) is hosted on one shared participant, where one ledger user can act as all of them. So **our code decides which party a seat acts as**:

- A seat route takes the party from the seat's current lease, never from the request; the `no-party-from-request` invariant fails the build otherwise.
- Everything a seat reads or acts on is bound to its current lease's start offset, so a recycled party never exposes a previous visitor's positions.

The Daml guarantees hold whoever submits: a Window resolves or voids exactly once, resolution needs the resolver and an oracle quorum, the venue cannot confiscate a position or accept a quote for you, and the `pm-tests` scripts check that money is conserved after every step.

## What runs today

On the local sandbox, each with an evidence note in `docs/evidence/`: calls on crypto, stock, xStock, pre-IPO, basket, Monday Gap and event Windows; Range, Moonshot, Parlay, Boost and shorts; Earn and the maker vault; strategies; duels, rank and seasons. Not yet: a desk that has traded live, private mode, the Canton Coin rail on a running ledger (it is proved in Daml Script and unit tests) and holdings-based cover; the iPhone app is built for Canton but has not run on a device.
