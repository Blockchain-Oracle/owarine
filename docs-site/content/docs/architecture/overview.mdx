title: "How Agari fits together"
description: "A prediction market on Canton Network: Daml contracts hold the money rules, parties hold the privacy, and demo credits are the only money."
---

Agari is a prediction market on **Canton Network** for short price calls: will a price close its Window up or down. The money rules live in a **Daml package** (`abu-pm-main`), not in the web app. The venue lists Windows and quotes a firm price for each side; you accept a quote from your seat; three oracle parties sign the prices; a separate resolver party settles each Window exactly once. Everything runs on a Canton test network with **demo credits** that are worth nothing anywhere else.

| Layer | Responsibility |
| --- | --- |
| Web app and phone app | Show markets, the ticket, positions, receipts and proof. Your seat's signing key lives in the browser (or the phone) and cannot be exported. |
| `packages/core` | Pure market, registry and money rules; integer arithmetic. |
| `packages/markets`, `packages/ledger` | The only code that talks to the Canton ledger: it builds Daml commands and reads contracts through the JSON Ledger API. |
| Daml package `abu-pm-main` | The contracts: Series, Window terms, oracle prints, quotes, positions (legs), cash and the resolution chain. See [The Daml engine](/architecture/programs). |
| Ops and the projector | Open Windows, post oracle prints, price and issue quotes, resolve, settle and fund seats; the projector turns the venue's ledger view into the tables the app reads. See [Ops and the projector](/architecture/ops-and-indexer). |

## Who can see what

Canton shows a contract only to the parties on it. A position (a `Leg`) is signed by the venue and its owner, so **only your seat's party and the venue can see it**. Another visitor, an oracle or the resolver does not. Prices and Window terms are shared with the parties that need them. The app's **Who can see this** notes and the view switcher on a market page show the same rule on screen.

## Seats instead of wallets

There is no wallet app to install. **Take a seat** makes a signing key in your browser that cannot leave it, and the venue leases you a Canton party of your own, funded with demo credits. A seat is released after it has been idle and has no open positions; before it is reused, its positions settle and its cash is swept, so the next visitor starts from an empty party. Canton charges no network fee, so there is no gas to buy. [Your seat](/start/wallet) has the details.

## One money path today

**Predict:** demo credits on a Canton test network. You accept a firm quote; the stake and a fee are locked in your position; the Window settles from the oracle parties' signed prints. A void returns the stake and the fee.

**Planned:** the desk's live leg, holder cover that reads real holdings, and the other products (Range, Boost, Parlay, Earn, games) are being ported onto the same contracts. Until each one lands, its screens say so. A read-only visitor can browse markets and proof without a seat.
