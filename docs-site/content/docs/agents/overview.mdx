**Automate** in the rail opens <AppLink href="/strategies">Strategies</AppLink>. A strategy describes how a runner should choose Up, Down or Hold. Publishing registers it; a follower's separately funded copy permission enables trading.

## Create a strategy

1. Open the creator studio. In **Identity & approach**, name the agent and choose an approach: model agent, momentum, reversion or mirror. Choose its portrait.
2. In **Behavior & limits**, write the brief or tune the rule. Set the most per trade and per day. The daily limit must cover one trade.
3. In **Test read**, run a model read for a model agent. It signs nothing and trades nothing. Rules show a preview rather than a live model result. Changing behavior or limits clears a previous model result.
4. In **Publish**, choose the configured house runner or name your own running bot, review the subscription fee and public playbook, and publish from your seat.
5. Wait for the publication receipt, then open **Your strategies**. [Set up a funded copy](/agents/copy-strategies) if you want the runner to act for this seat.

A successful test read is optional for publication. A strategy still needs eligible markets, its running process and, for a model approach, a working model credential. A **Hold** is a valid decision. Only a fill receipt establishes a trade. Your name, brief, playbook and runner address are public; keep credentials out of them.

## Choose the right automation

| Product | What it does | Permission |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Sensei](/explore/sensei) | Explains context and offers a card for you to review | Places no trade itself |
| Strategy copy | A named runner places calls inside funded limits | Revocable `AgentGrant` |
| [Desk](/agents/desk) | Maintains a chosen pre-IPO basket over time | Paper practice first; a separate mandate for DevNet trading |
| [Trade from X](/explore/trade-from-x) | Routes supported mentions to your allocated balance | Separate X link and trading permission |

<AppLink href="/agents">Agents</AppLink> lists runners from the registry and confirmed copy-trade receipts. A listed runner or published strategy is not a promise of activity or returns. Read the current deployment state and each permission before funding it.
