title: "Agents and strategies"
description: "Published strategies, bounded copy grants, Sensei and the pre-IPO desk do different jobs."
---

Owarine has two agent paths. <AppLink href="/agents">Agents</AppLink> and <AppLink href="/strategies">Strategies</AppLink> publish or copy Up/Down calls through a bounded grant your seat funds and may revoke. The [desk](/agents/desk) is a separate pre-IPO holding flow: paper practice first, then a live Daml mandate. Both run on a local Canton sandbox, not yet on a shared Canton network.

| Product | What it can do | Network and money |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sensei | Explains a ticker or position and hands you a call card. It does not place the call. | Reads the app; places nothing. Needs a model key on the server |
| Published strategy / copy | Follows (or fades) a creator's strategy through an `AgentGrant` your seat funds; the runner places calls only inside its caps. | Demo credits |
| Desk | Holds a chosen pre-IPO basket inside owner-set limits; the runner decides timing. | Practice is paper; the live mandate has not traded yet |

A listed strategy needs a running runner and a valid grant; read the app's current state before enabling a copy. The runner never sees your cash, and every position it opens is yours. The desk's [first-use guide](/agents/desk) and [trust boundary](/architecture/desk) explain its separate permissions. None of these pages is investment advice.
