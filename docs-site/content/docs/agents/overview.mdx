Agari has two agent paths. <AppLink href="/agents">Agents</AppLink> and <AppLink href="/strategies">Strategies</AppLink> publish or copy Up/Down activity through bounded grants. The [desk](/agents/desk) is a separate PreStocks holding flow: paper practice only; a live desk on Canton is planned.

| Product | What it can do | Network and money |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sensei | Explains a ticker or position and hands you a call card. It does not place the call. | Reads the app; places nothing |
| Published strategy / copy | Follows a creator's strategy through the grant the copier signs and may revoke. | Planned on Canton: the `AgentGrant` contract exists in the engine; the strategy runner is not wired yet |
| Desk | Holds a chosen PreStocks basket inside owner-set limits; the runner decides timing. | Paper practice; the live leg is planned |

A listed strategy needs a live runner and a valid grant, and neither runs on Canton yet. Read the app's current state before enabling a copy. The desk's [first-use guide](/agents/desk) and [trust boundary](/architecture/desk) explain its separate permissions. None of these pages is investment advice.
