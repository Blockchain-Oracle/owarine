---
title: "Copy a strategy"
description: "Review a creator, fund a ledger-enforced grant, and release it when you stop."
---

<AppLink href="/strategies">Strategies</AppLink> are published by creators; <AppLink href="/agents">Agents</AppLink> ranks the runners by the capital entrusted to them and the copy-trades they executed. A published strategy is not a promise of a fill or return. Check its runner, history, fee, playbook and hard caps before copying.

<GuideCapture name="strategyPublished" caption="A strategy just published from a seat. Publishing registers it and links its ledger transaction; it deposits nothing and enables no trade." />

To start, open the copy drawer and enter a **Total budget** and the **Most per trade** (no more than the strategy's own per-trade limit). The drawer then states the whole permission: per trade, per day, open positions and any entry-price ceiling, within the strategy's published caps, for 30 days. A strip shows your seat's credits and what this setup takes from them, fee included. **Max** fills the budget and the per-trade amount. Each field says in red what is wrong with it (for example "Above this strategy's … per trade" or "More than your total budget"), and the confirm button names the one rule holding it. **Fund permission and copy** then takes two separate steps from your seat: it moves the budget out of your cash into an `AgentGrant` that names the runner, and it subscribes you, paying the strategy's fee. If one step is uncertain, the app reconciles it before continuing; do not blindly repeat a grant. The runner may place calls only inside the grant: the ledger choice that places them (`Grant_AcceptQuote`) checks the price, per-trade, daily, budget and open-position caps itself. The runner never sees your cash, and each position is yours.

You can **copy** the creator's side or **fade** it by taking the opposite side of the same Window. A seat holds one strategy permission at a time: copying another strategy replaces this one's permission. **Add budget without changing limits** tops the grant up and keeps what it has already spent today. **Pause future copies** revokes the grant and ends the subscription: the whole unspent budget returns to your cash, and existing positions settle normally, with any payout landing in your seat's cash. If the strategy's runner changes, the grant still names the old one, so you authorize again; the budget has not moved. [The Daml engine](/architecture/programs) describes `AgentGrant`.

Strategies trade **every Window that is trading now**, on every lane: stocks in session and the 24/7 crypto, xStock, pre-IPO and basket Windows around the clock. The runner rests only when no Window trades at all: it checks every five minutes, makes no model call, and the strategy page reports **Resting**.

This is a **demo-credit** feature. Publish, copy, fade, top-up, a runner fill, settlement and revoke all ran through these screens on a local Canton sandbox; it is not on a shared Canton network yet. The creator's fee collects into a payout contract, but no screen claims it yet. A listing can be visible while its runner is unavailable; the app reports those states rather than inventing recent trades.
