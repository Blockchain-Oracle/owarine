The desk studio is the **Hold** path from [Baskets](/trading/baskets). You choose which PreStocks names it may hold and how much cash to keep. The desk decides *when* to act, within your limits. A desk is different from an Up/Down call: it holds tokens rather than predicting a Window's close.

<AppLink href="/desk">Desk</AppLink> without a desk of your own shows what a desk does in three steps, a live card of a desk its owner shares (its basket, total value and latest check), **Start a practice desk** and **See a shared desk**. No seat is needed until the last step of the studio.

<ConnectedWalkthrough name="desk" />

<Steps>
<Step>
### Choose the basket

Open <AppLink href="/desk/new">New desk</AppLink>, or choose **Hold** on a basket card. **The basket** step shows the five baskets as cards with their members and a seven-day line, plus **Build your own** from any of the eight PreStocks companies. Adjust each weight and the cash share with sliders; **Even split** and **Reset to basket** undo your edits, and the weights and cash must add up to 100%. Anyone can draft without a seat. The draft stays in this browser.

<GuideCapture name="studioCurrent" caption="The current studio begins with five basket cards or Build your own. AI Labs starts at 40% OpenAI, 40% Anthropic and 20% cash, all editable draft values." />
</Step>
<Step>
### Set limits

**How strict** starts from **Careful**, **Balanced** or **Loose** (or **Custom**) and puts every limit on a slider: allowed drift, the largest share one company may take, the loss stop, the premium ceiling above the PreStocks mark, the per-action and daily spend caps, and the size above which it asks first. Each limit says who enforces it: the per-action and daily caps, the premium ceiling and the allowed companies would be **enforced by the ledger** once the live desk exists (planned); drift, the largest share and the loss stop are **enforced by the desk**'s own code. The defaults are Balanced: 5% drift, 50% largest share, a 15% loss stop, a 10% premium ceiling, $50 per action, $150 a day and ask first above $100.

<GuideCapture name="limitsCurrent" caption="The slider view labels the premium ceiling as a ledger rule for a future live desk, and drift, concentration and loss stop as desk-code checks." />
</Step>
<Step>
### Ask for a test read

**Test read** runs one real check and shows it as it happens: sign the mandate, read PreStocks prices for every company, price each trade at your size, ask the one timing question, then write the decision down and fingerprint it. This asks your seat to sign a message; it does **not** move money. A bad or stale quote leads to a recorded refusal, not a trade. You can also continue without a test read.

<GuideCapture name="testReadCurrent" caption="The step previews the PreStocks and timing sequence. This public capture stops before signing; no test read ran and the amount is paper practice money." />
</Step>
<Step>
### Create the practice desk

**Create** shows a receipt of the split, the limits and where the desk starts before you sign. One signed message from your seat creates a paper desk. Practice runs the same decision pipeline on real prices and quotes every hour, on the hour, with a paper cash ledger and **no ledger transaction**. The app requires six practice checks of your own and a visit to the whole record before it offers Go live.

<GuideCapture name="createCurrent" caption="The final step awaits the owner's signed message. This capture did not create another desk." />
</Step>
</Steps>

## Read your desk

Your desk's page opens on its value: a chart of the desk's worth at every check (**1D**, **1W**, **All**), the next check and how many practice checks are done. Four tabs sit under it:

<GuideCapture name="deskOverview" caption="A connected owner sees a Frontier AI practice desk, its paper value chart and six completed checks. This is paper money, not a real balance." />

| Tab | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Overview** | The latest decision, the allocation now against the target, and the limits in use (spent today, highest premium, distance from the loss stop). |
| **Holdings** | Each company's tokens, its target share and its recent prices, then the cash. |
| **Activity** | Every check as a timeline, newest first, with day headings, quiet runs folded together and filters: **All**, **Acted**, **Decided not to**, **Asked you**, **Quiet**, **Problems**. |
| **Rules** | The basket and each limit, marked as a ledger rule (for the planned live desk) or **Enforced by the desk**, with **Edit** to sign a new mandate version. |

Open any check to read its decision page: the verdict, the companies, how sure the timing call was, the price strip (token price, PreStocks mark, 30-minute average, Pyth index when one was read, and your premium ceiling), the trade it chose or turned down, and each check it passed or refused. **Check it** recomputes the record's fingerprint. A desk its owner has not shared reads as private to anyone else.

## Go live is planned

On Canton, **Go live** is planned as a desk mandate built on the engine's agent grant: your seat would move a budget into it, and the ledger would check every action against your limits. The live leg would trade this venue's own markets with venue cash, and only once the Canton Coin rail lands. There is no real-money desk today, and no external exchange route. Do not treat a paper balance as a real one.

The desk's [architecture and trust boundary](/architecture/desk) explains what the program checks and what the venue attests.
