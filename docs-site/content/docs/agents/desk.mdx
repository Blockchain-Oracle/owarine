title: "Build a desk"
description: "Choose a pre-IPO basket and limits, run a paper practice desk, then open a live desk as a Daml mandate."
---

The desk studio is the **Automate** path from [Baskets](/trading/baskets). You choose which of the eight pre-IPO companies it may hold and how much cash to keep. The desk decides *when* to act, within your limits. A desk is different from a single Up/Down call: it keeps a target mix and rebalances it over time.

<AppLink href="/desk">Desk</AppLink> without a desk of your own shows what a desk does in three steps, a card of a desk its owner shares on this deployment (its basket, total value and latest check), **Start a practice desk** and **See a shared desk**. No seat is needed until the last step of the studio.

<Steps>
<Step>
### Choose the basket

Open <AppLink href="/desk/new">New desk</AppLink>, or choose **Automate** on a basket card. **The basket** step shows the five baskets as cards with their members and a seven-day line, plus **Build your own** from any of the eight PreStocks companies. Adjust each weight and the cash share with sliders; **Even split** and **Reset to basket** undo your edits, and the weights and cash must add up to 100%. Anyone can draft without a seat. The draft stays in this browser.

</Step>
<Step>
### Set limits

**How strict** starts from **Careful**, **Balanced** or **Loose** (or **Custom**) and puts every limit on a slider: allowed drift, the largest share one company may take, the loss stop, the premium ceiling above a company's mark, the per-action and daily spend caps, and the size above which it asks first. Each limit says who enforces it: on a live desk the per-action and daily caps, the premium ceiling and the allowed companies are **Enforced on the ledger** by the mandate; drift, the largest share and the loss stop are **Enforced by the desk**'s own code. The defaults are Balanced: 5% drift, 50% largest share, a 15% loss stop, a 10% premium ceiling, 50 per action, 150 a day and ask first above 100 (paper dollars in practice, demo credits on a live desk).

</Step>
<Step>
### Ask for a test read

**Test read** runs one real check and shows it as it happens: sign the mandate, read PreStocks prices for every company, price each trade at your size, ask the one timing question, then write the decision down and fingerprint it. This asks your seat to sign a message; it does **not** move money. A bad or stale price leads to a recorded refusal, not a trade. The timing question needs a model key on the server; without one the check records **Could not decide** and names the missing setting. You can also continue without a test read.

</Step>
<Step>
### Create the practice desk

**Create** shows a receipt of the split, the limits and where the desk starts before you sign. One signed message from your seat creates a paper desk. Practice runs the same decision pipeline on real prices every hour, on the hour, with a paper cash ledger and **no ledger write**. **Check now** asks for an extra check, at most once every ten minutes. Go live unlocks after six hourly practice checks of your own and a visit to the whole record.

</Step>
</Steps>

## Read your desk

Your desk's page opens on its value: a chart of the desk's worth at every check (**1D**, **1W**, **All**), the next check and how many practice checks are done. Four tabs sit under it:

<GuideCapture name="deskPractice" caption="An AI Labs practice desk: paper money, the next hourly check, and 1 of 6 practice checks done before Go live." />

| Tab | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Overview** | The latest decision, the allocation now against the target, and the limits in use (spent today, highest premium, distance from the loss stop). |
| **Holdings** | Each company's holding (paper tokens in practice; Up lots on its hourly Window on a live desk), its target share and its recent prices, then the cash. |
| **Activity** | Every check as a timeline, newest first, with day headings, quiet runs folded together and filters: **All**, **Acted**, **Decided not to**, **Asked you**, **Quiet**, **Problems**. |
| **Rules** | The basket and each limit, marked **Enforced on the ledger** or **Enforced by the desk**, with **Edit** to sign a new mandate version. |

Open any check to read its decision page: the verdict, the companies, how sure the timing call was, the price strip (token price, PreStocks mark, 30-minute average and your premium ceiling), the trade it chose or turned down, and each check it passed or refused. **Check it** recomputes the record's fingerprint. A desk its owner has not shared reads as private to anyone else; **Share** turns on a read-only link that shows holdings and the record, never your notes.


## Go live

**Go live** opens a `DeskMandate` on Canton from your seat: you allow the companies, link the record and add money from your seat's demo credits. The mandate trades this venue's own markets with that cash: holding a company means holding Up lots on its hourly pre-IPO Window, bought at the venue's firm price and sold back through the venue's buy-back quote (never below 92% of the attested value). Every buy passes the mandate's embedded grant caps and a premium ceiling over marks the oracle parties post, and every action, a quiet checkpoint included, extends a hash chain of `DeskDecision` records on the ledger. You or the runner may **Pause** it; only you can **Resume**, change the limits, withdraw or close it.

On a local Canton sandbox, opening a live desk, adding money, pausing and resuming, a sealed checkpoint and the shared read-only desk all ran through the screens. The live desk has not traded there yet: no model key was set, so every check ended **Could not decide**, and its buy and sell steps are covered by the Daml tests instead. A live desk's value line still mixes in its practice record, a known gap. Nothing is on a shared Canton network yet, and there is no external exchange route. Do not treat a paper balance as a real one.
