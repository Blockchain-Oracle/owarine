<AppLink href="/status">Status</AppLink> reports the Canton ledger end, the projector's lag behind it, each oracle party's newest close, the resolver's and settler's backlogs, the heartbeat of every ops actor, and the guest-seat pool (free, leased, draining and waiting). Other rows cover the price sources behind the stock, xStock and pre-IPO lanes, and the Pyth valuation-index row, which shows whether the venue's key may read OpenAI and Anthropic's indices. While that entitlement is refused, the valuation lanes stay unlisted.

If a page you're following shows a stale reading, "signed price stale," or a seat that isn't being funded, check Status first. If everything there looks healthy and the problem persists, it's more likely something in the guide is out of date than the app itself.
