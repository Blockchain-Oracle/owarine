<AppLink href="/status">Status</AppLink> reports the ops actors, the oracle feeders and price pipelines, and the projector's freshness. The Pyth valuation-index row also shows whether the venue key may read OpenAI and Anthropic's indices. While the entitlement is refused, the corresponding valuation lanes stay unlisted.

If a page you're following shows a stale reading, "signed price stale," or a seat that isn't being funded, check Status first. If everything there looks healthy and the problem persists, it's more likely something in the guide is out of date than the app itself.
