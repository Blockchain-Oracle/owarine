## When something cannot act

Open <AppLink href="/status">Status</AppLink> and expand the affected service. A chart's latest price does not establish that the venue can offer a firm quote. A submitted request does not establish a fill; check Portfolio before retrying.

Missing or disagreeing boundary evidence can lead to a void. A recorded void refunds stake plus fee on settlement. If ops stays absent, the contract's stale-refund time matters; see [Halts and voids](/trading/halts-and-voids).

Dated acceptance records and the capability inventory live under `.github/verification/` in the repository. Their `live` state means recorded DevNet execution, not uninterrupted availability.
