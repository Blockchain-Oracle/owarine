## All guest seats are taken

Seats come from a small shared pool. The page shows your place in line and when the next seat frees, and takes it for you; browsing and proof stay open meanwhile.

## My seat is gone

A seat idle for 15 minutes with no open position or live quote goes back to the pool, and **Reset seat** deletes this browser's key for good. Take a new seat; it starts from fresh demo credits. Idle time does not count while a position is open.

## My other device won't join

A seat-link code lasts 60 seconds and works once, and the device that holds the seat must choose **Allow** within 45 seconds. If it expired, was used, or collected too many wrong guesses, choose **Show a new code** on the first device. A device that already holds its own seat cannot join another one.

## A call's outcome is unclear

A signed call is not the same as a filled one. Wait for the ticket's result, or check Portfolio, before retrying: retrying a call that actually landed can open a second position. A firm quote is held for about 20 seconds; an expired quote fills nothing.

## A Window shows "paused: no signed source"

This is expected, not a bug. A lane lists a Window only when a registered price source can be attested for it. If the source is down or refuses the venue's host (PreStocks rate-limits it at times), the lane pauses honestly and the card names the reason, rather than trading on an unverified price.

## A Window voided instead of settling

Either fewer than two of the three oracle parties posted a print in time, or their prints were more than 1% apart. Either way your stake and your fee return; see [halts and voids](/trading/halts-and-voids). This is not a lost trade.

## A feature seems to be missing entirely

Check [what's available](/help/availability) and <AppLink href="/status">Status</AppLink>. Some screens wait on a key or a service this deployment does not have, and they say so.
