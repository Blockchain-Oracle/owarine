## All guest seats are taken

Seats come from a small shared pool. The page says when the next one frees and takes it for you; browsing and proof stay open meanwhile.

## My seat is gone

An idle seat with no open positions goes back to the pool, and **Reset seat** deletes this browser's key for good. Take a new seat; it starts from fresh demo credits. A seat with open positions is never released before they settle.

## A call's outcome is unclear

A signed call is not the same as a filled one. Wait for the ticket's result, or check Portfolio, before retrying — retrying a call that actually landed can open a second position. A quote is valid for a few seconds; an expired quote fills nothing.

## A Window shows "paused: no signed source"

This is expected, not a bug. A Series only lists a Window when a registered price policy version covers both its opening and closing boundary. If none does, the lane pauses honestly rather than trading on an unverified price.

## A Window voided instead of settling

Either fewer than the quorum of oracle prints arrived in time, or the oracle quotes disagreed beyond the Series' limit. Either way your stake and your fee return — see [halts and voids](/trading/halts-and-voids). This is not a lost trade.

## A feature seems to be missing entirely

Check [what's available](/help/availability) and <AppLink href="/status">Status</AppLink>. Several products are still being ported to Canton, and their screens say so.
